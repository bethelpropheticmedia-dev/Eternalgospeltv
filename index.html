WHAT WE DO

Eternal Gospel TV

Eternal Gospel TV exists to provide a faith-filled, inspiring, and enriching viewing experience. Together, let's grow in faith and spread the love of Christ to the world.

At Eternal Gospel TV, we are dedicated to delivering inspiring, faith-based content that uplifts, encourages, and strengthens your spiritual journey.

Our programming is rooted in biblical teachings and designed to bring you closer to God. We offer a wide range of programs, including worship services, sermons, Bible studies, Christian talk shows, children's programming, prayer, inspirational messages, music, and more.

OUR FOUNDER

A Life Dedicated
to the Gospel

Pr. Founder Name

The journey of Bethel Prophetic Ministries is inseparable from the life and calling of Bishop John F. Aruldoss. His spiritual walk began in 1972 when he received salvation, marking the start of a lifelong commitment to the Kingdom of God. In 1983, following a powerful anointing, he officially entered the ministry.

Through Eternal Gospel TV, this vision continues to reach families and communities with faith, hope, worship, prayer, and the love of Christ.
